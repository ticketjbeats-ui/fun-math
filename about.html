Fun Math

For teachers and parents

Fun Math is a free learning website for elementary students in grades 1–5. Students practice math facts, word problems and reading through short activities that give instant feedback and help when an answer is wrong.

✅ Free, no ads
✅ No sign-up or accounts
✅ No personal data collected
✅ No chat or messaging
✅ Works on Chromebooks
✅ English and Spanish

Students choose a level (Starter, Builder, Pro or Challenge). The site adjusts number ranges, story grade and sight word lists to that level. Every activity can be read aloud.

Privacy and safety

  • Students type a first name or nickname and pick a picture. This, along with stars and progress, is saved only in the browser on that device. It is never sent to a server.
  • There are no accounts, logins, ads, chat, links to other websites, social features or tracking tools.
  • All fonts, pictures and audio load from this website. No third-party services are used.

Read the full privacy notice.

For school technology staff

Fun Math is a single static website. It loads everything from one domain:

this site’s domain

  • Allowing that one domain is enough. It needs no other domains, cookies or third-party scripts.
  • Suggested filter category: Education.
  • Works in Chrome on managed Chromebooks, with a touchscreen or keyboard (number keys work in math games). Students can also install it as an app from Chrome’s menu.
  • Progress is stored in the browser’s local storage, so it stays on the Chromebook a student uses.
