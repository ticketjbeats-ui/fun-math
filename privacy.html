Fun Math

Privacy notice

Last updated November 2, 2026

Fun Math is built for children. It is designed so that we do not collect personal information from anyone.

What stays on your device

When a student adds a player, the name or nickname they type, the picture they pick, and their stars, scores and practice history are saved in the web browser’s local storage on that device. This information is never sent to us or anyone else. To remove it, delete the player in Fun Math or clear the browser’s site data.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts, logins or email addresses
  • No ads, analytics, tracking pixels or tracking cookies
  • No chat, messaging or sharing between students
  • No third-party scripts. Fonts, pictures and audio all load from this website.

Read-aloud

Most read-aloud audio is recorded and loads from this website. For some text, such as math questions, Fun Math uses the speech voice built into the device or browser. That speech is handled by the device, and Fun Math does not record or send it anywhere.

Web server logs

Like most websites, the server that hosts Fun Math may keep standard technical logs: the IP address, time, and page or file requested. These logs are used only to keep the site running and secure. They are not used to identify students and are not shared or sold.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top will change too.
